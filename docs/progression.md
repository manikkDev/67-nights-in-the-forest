# World Progression

The game world expands as you upgrade the campfire, unlocking new areas to explore and rescue opportunities.

## Campfire Levels

The campfire starts at Level 1 and can be upgraded by adding fuel. Each level increases the accessible radius around the campfire.

| Level | Access Radius | Safe Zone Radius |
|-------|--------------|-----------------|
| 1     | 250 studs    | 50 studs        |
| 2     | 500 studs    | 100 studs       |
| 3     | 750 studs    | 150 studs       |
| 4     | 1000 studs   | 200 studs       |
| 5     | 1250 studs   | 250 studs       |
| 6     | 1600 studs   | 300 studs       |

## Rescue Unlocks

Each rescue becomes available at a specific campfire level:

- **Dino**: Level 2
- **Kraken**: Level 3
- **Squid**: Level 4
- **Koala**: Level 5

## World Boundary

A wall expands outward as the campfire levels up, marking the edge of the accessible area. Attempting to cross the boundary triggers a warning effect and pushes you back toward the campfire.

## Map Size

The full map is 3200×3200 studs, centered near (-75, -7). The distance from the center to the edge is 1600 studs.
