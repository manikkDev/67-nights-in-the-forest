# Owl

Owls appear during the night and swoop at players near the campfire.

## Stats

- HP: 80
- Damage: 20 per attack
- Movement: Flies in circles, swoops at targets

## Behavior

- Circles the campfire during the night
- Swoops at players when in range
- Retreats briefly after an attack

## Flashlight Interaction

When a flashlight beam hits the owl's face:
- Freezes temporarily in place
- HUD "eye meter" indicates proximity (fills as owl approaches)
- Owl continues circling after the stun ends

## Loot

- Drops raw meat on death
- Cooked meat heals 15 HP (vs 3 HP raw)

## Strategy

- Watch the eye meter to anticipate owl attacks
- Use the flashlight to interrupt swoops
- Stay near the campfire for safety
