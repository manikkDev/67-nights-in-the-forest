# Crafting Guide

Crafting in the game converts raw resources into tools, structures, and items that help you survive the 67 nights.

## Resources

- **Wood**: Gathered by chopping trees with an axe
- **Stone**: Gathered by mining rocks
- **Scrap**: Gathered from junk piles scattered across the map
- **Berries**: Foraged from bushes (heals 5 HP)
- **Mushrooms**: Foraged from the forest floor (heals 6 HP)
- **Raw Meat**: Dropped by deer/owl (heals 3 HP raw, 15 HP cooked)

## Structures

Craft structures via the building UI (B key or D-pad):

- **Campfire**: Central survival hub. Provides light, warmth, and cooking.
- **Beds**: Save spawn points and restore health overnight.
- **Defenses**: Walls, spikes, and barriers to protect the campfire.
- **Utility**: Storage, crafting stations, and other camp improvements.

## Weapons

- **Spear**: Obtained from the Dino cave chest. Primary melee weapon.
- **Axe**: Used for chopping trees (may be craftable in future phases).

## Cooking

Cook raw meat at the campfire to significantly increase its healing value (3 HP → 15 HP).

## Tips

- Prioritize the campfire first — it's essential for cooking and warmth.
- Build beds early to ensure safe spawn points.
- Collect scrap whenever you see it — it's valuable for advanced structures.
